Design debt
The first design owed C40’s grid. This one owes Arounda’s port concept

This site was designed and built in conversation with Claude, who also drew the product loop on the homepage. The words describe real work and real products, and the opinions are all Venkatesh’s.

The rules the site is built under: no framework, no build step, no third-party requests, and text you can read at arm’s length. The current look is the fourth design system this site has worn. A flat ink grid on paper, a dark glass canvas and an earthy line-art phase all live on in the git history, and there is a field note about what that churn cost. Both were measured against the same accessibility numbers before shipping.
